The Corporate AV Gear That Actually Matters

A practical guide to building a reliable meeting and presentation setup without buying equipment you don't need.

Corporate AV gets complicated quickly. A dependable meeting does not require every gadget in the catalog. It requires the right tools, backups for predictable failure points, and equipment that works together.

1. Presentation control

A presentation remote is inexpensive compared with the rest of the room, but a failure is instantly visible. Prioritize dependable connectivity, usable range, simple controls and a backup plan.

2. Confidence monitoring

A properly positioned confidence monitor lets presenters follow slides or notes without repeatedly turning away from the audience. Size, placement and signal distribution matter more than buying the largest display available.

3. Audio people can actually understand

If the audience cannot clearly understand the presenter, the rest of the production is secondary. We'll separate recommendations for wireless handhelds, lavaliers, small mixers, speakers, interfaces and essential adapters.

Allen's rule: Build the audio chain for clarity and reliability first. Add complexity only when the event actually needs it.

4. Display & projection

Screen size and projector brightness should be matched to the room, ambient light, viewing distance and audience—not chosen from a single “best projector” list. Future guides will separate small meetings, medium conference rooms and larger corporate events.

5. The emergency AV kit

This is the inexpensive gear that can save an event: fresh batteries, known-good HDMI cables, USB-C/HDMI adapters, audio adapters, extension cables, power strips, gaff tape, a backup presentation remote and clearly labeled spares.
